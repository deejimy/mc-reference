# Restreint


A player cannot have more than two cards with the
restricted keyword in play under their control at the same
time.
A player can play or put into play a restricted card even
if they already control two restricted cards. However, if
a player ever controls more than two restricted cards in
play, they must immediately choose and discard from play
restricted cards they control until they have only two in play.


See also: Discard, Keywords, Ownership and Control,
Player, Reminder Text
