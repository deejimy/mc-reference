# Phase des joueurs


During the player phase, each player (in player order) takes
one turn.
After each player has taken a turn, the players discard down
or draw up to their hand size and ready each exhausted
card.

  - Effects that last "until the end of the [player] phase"
end after players draw up to their hand size and all
cards are readied. Then, effects that resolve "when/
after the player phase ends" are resolved.


See also: End of Player Phase, In Player Order, Player, Player
Turn
