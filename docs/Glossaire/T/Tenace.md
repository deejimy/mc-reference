# Tenace


Tough is a status that prevents a character from taking
damage.

  - If a character with a tough status card would take
any amount of damage, prevent all of that damage
and discard a tough status card from that character
instead.

  - A character with multiple tough status cards
discards only one tough status card each time it
would take damage.

  - When a hero with a tough status card defends an
attack, they reduce the damage from the attack by
their DEF first. If the damage is reduced to 0, the
hero does not lose their tough status card.

  - When an ally or minion with a tough status card is
dealt excess damage by an attack with the overkill
keyword, overkill damage is not dealt to the identity
of that ally’s controller or to the villain, respectively.

  - As a tough status card prevents damage fully, the
character who had the tough status card is not
considered to have taken damage.
