# Solide


Un personnage avec le mot-clé `solide` peut avoir deux cartes d'état sonné et deux désorienté. Il n'est affecté que s'il en possède deux du même type.

**Voir aussi** : Désorienté, Mots-clés, Cartes d'état, Sonné


```
STEADY
A character with the steady keyword can have one
additional stunned status card and one additional confused
status card. That character is not stunned unless they have
two stunned status cards, and is not confused unless they
have two confused status cards. After that character’s
attack, scheme, or thwart is canceled by a status card effect,
remove all status cards of the corresponding type from that
character.


See also: Confuse, Keywords, Status Cards, Stun
```
